# What to collect per case study

Six fields do the work. Everything else is derived.

1. **Trade and city.** "HVAC, Dallas TX".
2. **The search phrase** a customer would actually type, exactly. "ac repair dallas".
3. **Rank before and after**, plus the month each was measured. "8 in May, 1 in August".
4. **Calls before and after, per month**, from their Google Business Profile performance screen.
   Two numbers, not a percentage. The percentage is computed.
5. **Revenue growth in dollars**, as the owner gave it, plus the window. "$180,000 over a year".
6. **The quote, verbatim**, and their role. Do not tidy the grammar.

Then two housekeeping notes per case:

- **Where the quote came from**, so we can show it is genuine. "text message 2026-09-11" is enough.
- **Whether they received anything of value** for it. Almost certainly blank, but it has to be asked.

## The AI half, and the one thing worth doing before you send them

The section shows "3 of 3 AI engines name them, was 0 of 3". The after number I can check myself
right now, in about a minute per client. The BEFORE number cannot be recovered after the fact,
because nobody can ask ChatGPT what it would have said in May.

So for the four existing cases, the before state has to come from what we observed at the time or
from the owner. If neither exists for a case, that card drops the AI band metric and keeps the
red "never named them" only if we genuinely saw it. We do not guess it.

For every client from here on: run the scan at signup and keep it. That single habit turns every
future client into a case study with a real before.
