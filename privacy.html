Privacy

Effective August 13, 2026

Local Growth Partner is the local visibility practice of Golden Partners. This page explains, in plain words, what happens with your information on this website.

What we collect

Only what you type into the free scan form: your business name, city, phone number and email address. When you press the button, the form opens your own email app with that information already filled in, and you choose whether to send it. Nothing is collected until you do.

What we do with it

  • We use it to run your free visibility scan and to reply to you.
  • We do not sell it, rent it, or share it with anyone outside our team.
  • If you become a client, we keep it for as long as we work together, plus the time the law requires.

Cookies and tracking

We do not set cookies and we do not run analytics or advertising pixels on this site today. The page loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which means Google receives your IP address when the page loads; Google's handling of that is described in Google's privacy policy. If we add analytics or advertising tools, we will update this page first.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct, or delete any information we hold about you. Email ben@benjix.com and we will do it. California residents have these rights under the CCPA; everyone who writes to us gets the same treatment.

Questions
